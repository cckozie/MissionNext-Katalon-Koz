Assignment Description:   We are looking for a computer teacher to join our community of educators for the 2026-2027 school year. Current openings is for elementary and secondary computer. Ideal candidates are passionate about our mission to promote academic excellence in a Christ-centered environment and meet the following qualifications: -Teachers must have a personal relationship with Jesus Christ. -Teachers must have a bachelor's degree.
